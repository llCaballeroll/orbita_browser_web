Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

¿Prefieres español? Lee la política en español

This policy explains how Orbita – Wrist Browser (“Orbita”, “the app”), a web browser for Apple Watch with a companion iPhone app, developed by Luis Angel Caballero Jardón, handles your information.

In short

Orbita does not collect, store on servers or share any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, analytics, ads or trackers.

1. Data we collect

None. Orbita doesn’t send information about you, your device or your browsing to the developer or to third parties. The app includes no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting kits.

2. Information stored on your Apple Watch

To work, Orbita stores locally on your watch:

  • Your bookmarks, browsing history and open tabs.
  • Your settings: search engine, content blocking, allowed sites and player volume.
  • Cookies and data from the sites you visit, managed by the system’s web engine.

This information never leaves the watch. You can clear history and website data in Settings → Privacy, and deleting the app removes everything. Private Browsing saves no history, cookies or tabs.

3. The iPhone app

The iPhone app shows a guide and the optional tips. It doesn’t browse the web, doesn’t create an account and doesn’t collect data.

4. Websites you visit

When you open a page, your watch connects directly to that site, as in any browser. Each website, and the search engine you choose (DuckDuckGo, Google, Bing or Ecosia), has its own privacy policy and may receive data such as your IP address. Orbita doesn’t control those practices.

Orbita’s blocker reduces what those sites can collect: it blocks, on the watch, connections to known advertising and tracking domains. The blocklist ships inside the app and is applied without contacting any server.

5. Tips and purchases

Optional tips are processed through Apple’s in-app purchases on iPhone. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy; the developer doesn’t receive your name, email or payment details.

6. Children

Orbita doesn’t collect data from anyone, including minors. The content shown comes from the websites the user chooses to visit.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll publish the new version on this page with its update date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, write to soporte.orbitabrowser@outlook.com.
