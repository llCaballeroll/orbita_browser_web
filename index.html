Everyday browsing

Everything a browser needs.
Sized for your wrist.

Tabs

Keep several pages open and switch between them. They stay open after you close the app, and unused tabs go to sleep to save memory.

History and bookmarks

Find what you read again and save the sites you use most. Clear your history whenever you want.

Search by voice

Dictate, scribble or type. Say an address like “wikipedia dot org”. Choose DuckDuckGo, Google, Bing or Ecosia.

Mobile or desktop

Ask sites for their mobile, desktop or watch version, from Settings or the page menu.

Page zoom

Make text bigger or smaller from the page menu.

Pop-up blocking

Pop-ups are stopped, and the page menu tells you how many.

Privacy

Your browsing
stays on your watch.

Orbita has no accounts, no analytics and no ads of its own.

  • No data collectedNothing about you or your browsing is sent to the developer or to third parties.
  • Stored only on your watchBookmarks, history and tabs never leave it.
  • Private BrowsingSaves no history, cookies or tabs.
  • One-tap cleanupClear cookies and website data from Settings.
