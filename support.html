Frequently asked questions

Do I need my iPhone to browse?

No. Orbita works right on your Apple Watch over Wi‑Fi or cellular. Pages load fastest that way; through your iPhone’s Bluetooth they’re slower. The iPhone app is a quick guide and the place to leave a tip.

Where do I find Orbita?

Open it from the app list on your Apple Watch. The iPhone app doesn’t browse: it shows the guide and the tips.

How do I type an address?

Tap the search field on the Home screen and use dictation, Scribble or the keyboard. To dictate an address, say for example “wikipedia dot org”.

How do I change the volume or skip through a video?

When a video opens, turn the Digital Crown to change the volume. Tap the screen and choose “Crown: Seek” so the Crown moves through the video; you can also use the 10‑second buttons.

How do I listen on my AirPods?

In the player, tap the headphones icon and choose your AirPods or Bluetooth headphones. To bring the sound back to the watch, tap the watch icon.

A video won’t play

Apple Watch plays MP4 and HLS videos. Some sites use formats or protections the watch doesn’t support; in that case Orbita shows a notice. If a site’s player stops working, try turning off Settings → Video Compatibility.

A page looks wrong or is missing content

Open the page’s ••• menu and choose “Allow Ads on This Site”; some sites depend on scripts the blocker stops. You can also change the Site Version to Desktop or Mobile.

How do I clear my history or website data?

Go to Settings → Privacy and tap “Clear History” or “Clear Cookies & Website Data”. You can also turn on Private Browsing.

Which search engines can I use?

DuckDuckGo (the default), Google, Bing or Ecosia. Change it in Settings → Search.

What do tips include?

Nothing extra: tips are optional one-time payments to support development, and they don’t unlock features. You leave them from the iPhone app. If you had a problem with a charge, request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
